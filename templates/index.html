AI Job Shop Scheduler

Deep Reinforcement Learning Dashboard

ℹ️ About This Project

This dashboard demonstrates a Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) approach to solving the Job Shop Scheduling Problem (JSSP). JSSP is a complex, NP-Hard manufacturing optimization challenge where multiple jobs must be processed across various machines in the most time-efficient order possible.

🧠 The Agent (DQN)
A PyTorch Deep Q-Network that autonomously learns the best scheduling policy through thousands of trial-and-error episodes.
🎯 The Objective
Maximize the reward by minimizing the overall "makespan" (the total amount of time required to completely finish all operations).
⚙️ The Environment
A custom OpenAI Gym environment simulating factory constraints, machine availability, and job operation sequences.
